DEPSTAR                                     1                         Department of  Information Technology  ABSTRACT  
LiterSage is an AI -powered document intelligence platform designed to transform the 
traditional reading and research experience into an interactive, highly efficient process. The 
project aims to enhance comprehension and productivity by providing automated , deep 
insights into uploaded documents for both guest and member users. Key features include 
comprehensive Document Summarization, Key Point Extraction, Pain Point Identification, 
and automated Title Generation, leveraging a combination of fine -tuned loca l machine 
learning models and the Groq API for high -performance natural language processing. Built 
with React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion for a premium, futuristic frontend, and 
Node.js with Express for robust backend integration, LiterSage ensures a seamless and 
scalable solution. Registered members require accounts to access an exclusive, tabbed 
dashboard for deeper analysis, enhancing personalization and capabilities, while guest 
users can access limited core features. This innovative platform strea mlines literature 
review, improves reading efficiency, and modernizes complex document analysis.

DEPSTAR                                     2                         Department of  Information Technology  CHAPTER 1: INTRO DUCTION  
1.1: Background of the Project  
In recent years, the volume of digital literature, research papers, and complex 
documentation has grown exponentially, yet the traditional methods for reading and 
analyzing these texts remain largely manual and time -consuming. This leads to significant 
inefficiencies, such as information overload, difficulty extracting key insights, and a 
disconnected research experience for students, professionals, and avid readers. With the 
rapid advancement of artificial intelligence and natural language processing, user s now 
expect intelligent, automated, and highly efficient tools to rapidly digest, summarize, and 
understand extensive written content.  
 
1.2: Problem Definition:  
Many students, researchers, and professionals face challenges processing large volumes of 
complex literature, often struggling to manually extract key insights, summarize core 
concepts, or identify critical pain points in a timely manner. Users frequently encounter 
difficulties quickly navigating and comprehending dense documents, while traditional 
reading methods and existing tools lack the advanced, automated analytical capabilities 
required to efficiently streamline the research and learning process.  
 
1.3: Motivation for the Project:  
The motivation behind LiterSage: Literature Mastered is to bridge the gap between readers 
and dense, complex documents by providing an interactive, AI -powered document 
intelligence platform. The goal is to enhance the research and reading experience for 
students, professionals, and guest users by offering rapid, automated assistance for 
document summarization, key point extraction, pain point identification, and insightful 
content analysis.  
 
1.4: Objectives and Scope of the Project:  
• To provide an interactive document intelligence platform for seamless extraction of 
insights from complex texts.  
• To implement AI -powered models for automated document summarization, key point 
extraction, pain point identification, and title generation.

DEPSTAR                                     13                         Department of  Information Technology  CHAPTER 8: CHALLENGES FACED  
• Fine -Tuning AI Models on Local Hardware : Training and fine -tuning multiple 
NLP models (BART and FLAN -T5) locally on Apple Silicon required careful 
resource management. We encountered compatibility issues with PyTorch's Metal 
Performance Shaders (MPS), leading to crashes during unsupported operati ons, as 
well as strict memory constraints when orchestrating the unified training pipeline for 
four different models.  
• Managing Complex Document Parsing and Token Limits : Processing 
unstructured, real -world documents (PDFs, DOCX) resulted in messy, unformatted 
text strings. Feeding this raw data into the local Hugging Face models was difficult 
because models like bart -large -cnn have strict maximum token limits, which long 
documents easily exceeded.  
• Resolving Environment and Port Conflicts : During the integration of the Node.js 
backend and React frontend, we encountered severe environment issues, notably a 
port conflict where macOS AirPlay was occupying the default backend port (5000), 
preventing the API server from starting and disrupting cl ient-server communication.  
• UI/UX Consistency and CSS Styling Conflicts : Achieving the desired "premium, 
futuristic" aesthetic using Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion led to complex styling 
conflicts. Specifically, overriding default Tailwind utility classes caused unexpected 
layout shifts and rendering inconsistencies between the  Guest and Member 
dashboards.  
 
Solutions:  
• Configured PyTorch MPS CPU fallbacks and utilized PEFT to train models within 
local hardware constraints.  
• Implemented dynamic text chunking to prevent documents from exceeding Hugging 
Face model token limits.  
• Migrated the API server to port 5001 and updated the frontend proxy to bypass 
AirPlay port conflicts.

9.1: Conclusion  
LiterSage: Literature Mastered successfully achieves its goal of transforming the 
traditional, manual reading experience into an interactive, highly efficient AI -powered 
platform. It bridges the gap between users and complex literature, delivering rapid, 
automated extraction of document summaries, key points, pain points, and dynamic titles, 
all while ensuring secure, role -based tiered access and optimal research productivity.  
 
9.2: Future Scope  
• Mobile App development for cross -platform accessibility.  
• Integration of more advanced analytical modules, such as a 'Chat with Document' 
feature and sentiment analysis.  
• Voice -based read -aloud feature for document summaries and key points.  
• Multi -language support to process, translate, and summarize non -English literature.  
• Integration with cloud storage platforms (Google Drive, Dropbox) and academic 
databases for direct document import.
